
# SEO Checklist for Beginners

A practical SEO learning guide by Digispire Academy, Kolkata.

## 1. Keyword Research
- Find relevant keywords
- Analyze search intent
- Research competitor keywords

## 2. On-Page SEO
- Optimize meta titles and descriptions
- Use proper H1, H2 and H3 headings
- Add internal links
- Optimize image alt text

## 3. Technical SEO
- Check XML sitemap
- Review robots.txt
- Fix broken links
- Check mobile usability

## 4. Local SEO
- Optimize Google Business Profile
- Maintain accurate business listings
- Collect genuine customer reviews
